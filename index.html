Parameter count is not the same thing as RAM.

»See the models

Bars are the size of the file you load, as published: quantized GGUF for the on-device rows, bf16 safetensors for the rest (2 bytes per parameter). Context memory is extra and grows per token; Songgot-X keeps 12 KB per token at fp16 and 6.4 KB at q8_0 because only 6 of its 24 layers hold a growing cache.

Why Palette

AI that Korean work can actually run on.

Tool calls on a phone, documents on a workstation, video in the cloud. One family, Korean-first, open where it can be, measured everywhere.

  • 1Number one in Korean on-device tool calling. Songgot-X leads every measured model, including the two built by Korea's largest AI labs, and it fits in a browser tab.
  • 2A 14B that beats a 32B. Palette-K-14B and Palette-K-Midm lead PALETTE-BENCH-KO on Korean enterprise documents ahead of Qwen, Kakao and Naver models twice their size.
  • 3Best open Korean speech on mixed-language audio. Palette STT leads the HiKE code-switch benchmark among open systems.
  • 4Korean text that survives generation. Hangul inside images, video and try-on photos, verified by OCR before it ships.
  • 5Runs where your data is. Offline on the device, on-prem in your rack, or hosted in Korea and Singapore. Apache 2.0 on every published weight.
